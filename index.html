My Vision for Alpha Phi Alpha

A Stronger, More Unified Arkansas District

My vision is to build a stronger, more unified Arkansas District by promoting leadership development, chapter excellence, brotherhood, and community impact. I will foster collaboration between alumni and collegiate chapters, strengthen mentorship, increase training on fraternity policies and best practices, and expand scholarship and service initiatives. I am committed to transparent communication, accountability, and supporting every chapter with the resources needed to thrive. Together, we will preserve Alpha’s rich legacy while preparing the next generation of leaders, ensuring our district continues to exemplify the fraternity’s aims of scholarship, fellowship, good character, and the uplifting of humanity.

Meet the Candidate

Dr. Marshawn C. Wilbourn, Sr.

“God Will and God Can”

For over 20 years, Dr. Wilbourn has served Alpha Phi Alpha at every level — from chapter president and Dean of Membership Intake to Arkansas Area 5 Director, Arkansas District Chaplain, and regional and national committee service.

A brother of Kappa Psi Chapter at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock (initiated 1999), he currently serves as President of Tau Phi Lambda Chapter. Professionally, he is a Veterans Service Representative with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, a U.S. Army veteran, and a pastor — bringing faith, discipline, and a servant’s heart to everything he does.
